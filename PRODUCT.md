# HOVERBOARD V1 Product Requirements

## Product

HOVERBOARD is a DJ gig-booking platform.

**Slogan:** “Find the Gig. It’s on the Board.”

## Problem

Clients need a simple way to discover and book DJs. DJs need a simple way to receive, manage, and get paid for gigs.

## V1 Goal

Make it easy for a client to find, book, and pay a DJ while giving the DJ a straightforward way to manage bookings and earnings.

## User Roles

HOVERBOARD V1 has exactly two roles:

- Solo DJ
- Client

## Solo DJ Capabilities

- Create an account
- Create and edit a DJ profile
- Add DJ name, photo, bio, location, genres, pricing, and availability
- Receive booking requests
- Accept or decline requests
- View upcoming gigs
- View booking history
- View earnings
- Receive payment after completing gigs
- Receive client reviews

## Client Capabilities

- Create an account
- Create and edit a client profile
- Search DJs
- Filter DJs
- View DJ profiles
- View pricing and availability
- Request bookings
- Pay for bookings
- View upcoming bookings
- View booking history
- Review DJs

## Booking Lifecycle

`requested → accepted/declined → paid → confirmed → completed`

A cancellation state may also exist where appropriate.

## V1 Non-Goals

DJ Teams, clubs/venues, other performer types, GPS verification, QR verification, advanced reputation, AI matching, payroll, recurring club bookings, complex disputes, team payout splitting, emergency replacement, equipment marketplace, advanced analytics, and broader performer marketplace expansion are not V1 requirements.

## Product Principles

- Simple before sophisticated.
- Trust and security from the beginning.
- Clear pricing and booking status.
- One major feature at a time.
- Working V1 is more important than a huge feature list.
