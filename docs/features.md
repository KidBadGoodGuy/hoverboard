# HOVERBOARD V1 Features

## V1 Features

### Account & Profiles

- DJ account creation and authentication
- Client account creation and authentication
- DJ profile creation and editing
- Client profile creation and editing

### DJ Discovery

- DJ search
- DJ filtering
- Public DJ profiles
- Pricing display
- Availability display

### Bookings

- Booking date/time selection
- Booking requests
- DJ accept/decline
- Upcoming bookings
- Booking history
- Booking status tracking

### Payments

- Client payment through Stripe
- Payment status tracking
- DJ payment after completed gigs

### Reviews

- Client ratings
- Client written reviews
- Reviews displayed on DJ profiles

## Explicitly Not V1

- DJ Teams
- Club/Venue accounts
- Other performer types
- GPS verification
- QR check-in/check-out
- Advanced reputation systems
- AI performer matching
- Payroll
- Recurring club bookings
- Complex dispute systems
- Automatic team payout splitting
- Emergency DJ replacement
- Equipment marketplace
- Advanced analytics
- Performer marketplace expansion

A feature may only move into V1 after an explicit product decision.
