# HOVERBOARD V1 UI Guidelines

## Brand

**HOVERBOARD**

**Slogan:** “Find the Gig. It’s on the Board.”

## Desired Feel

The interface should feel:

- Modern
- Energetic
- Professional
- DJ-focused
- Easy to understand
- Trustworthy

## UX Principles

1. Keep navigation simple.
2. Make primary actions obvious.
3. Keep booking status easy to understand.
4. Show pricing and important booking information clearly.
5. Avoid unnecessary screens and complexity.
6. Design for accessibility and readable text.
7. Give users clear feedback after important actions.
8. Keep the experience consistent between DJ and client areas.

## Core Language

Use clear marketplace language such as:

- Find a DJ
- View Profile
- Request Booking
- Accept Booking
- Decline Booking
- Pay Now
- Upcoming Gigs
- Booking History
- Leave a Review

## Brand Language

Possible phrases include:

- “Check the Board.”
- “Get on the Board.”
- “What’s on the Board?”
- “Your next gig is on the Board.”

Use brand language naturally. Do not sacrifice clarity for clever wording.

## V1 Scope

Do not design screens for future roles or advanced features unless they are explicitly being planned for a later version.
